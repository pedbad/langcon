You are about to complete a diagnostic assessment designed to evaluate your English language proficiency and discipline-specific academic literacy as part of your application for graduate study at the University of Cambridge.

Please begin by completing your profile. Once this is done, the assessment will be unlocked in your dashboard.

The assessment consists of three sections:

{% icon 'ink_pen' class_='h-6 w-6 text-writing' %} Writing {% icon 'headphones' class_='h-6 w-6 text-listening' %} Listening {% icon 'book_open' class_='h-6 w-6 text-reading' %} Reading

There is no time limit, but we recommend that you spend no more than three hours in total. At the end of each section, you will be required to submit your response. Once submitted, responses cannot be edited. However, you may exit the assessment at any point and return later to complete any remaining sections.

You are expected to complete the assessment as soon as possible after receiving it from the ADTIS Administrator, preferably within 24 hours. All work must be entirely your own. It should not be checked or edited by anyone else, nor should it be copied from work you have previously written.

Your responses will be reviewed by the ADTIS Director. You may also be contacted to complete a follow-up Speaking assessment via Zoom. If required, you will receive an email with further details.

Based on your performance, the Language Centre will make a recommendation regarding your Language Condition to your admitting body (Postgraduate Admissions Office, Judge Business School, or Department). Information on the range of outcomes can be found on the Language Centre website: https://www.langcen.cam.ac.uk/adtis/graduate-applicants.html
